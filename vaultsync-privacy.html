Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | VaultSync v1.0.0

1. Core Philosophy: 100% Offline & Zero Telemetry

VaultSync is architected on an uncompromising privacy foundation: we collect, transmit, and store zero personal data on external servers. The application contains no analytics tracking SDKs, no advertisement frameworks, and no telemetry pings. Your media and encryption credentials remain strictly within your physical possession.

2. Hardware-Backed Encryption

All private photos, videos, and documents moved into the vault are encrypted using AES-256-GCM envelope encryption. Cryptographic keys are generated and sealed within your device's hardware-backed Android KeyStore (StrongBox or Trusted Execution Environment). ShintoPC Labs possesses no backdoor, no decryption keys, and no ability to access your locked data.

3. In-Memory Decryption & Flash Storage Protection

When you preview videos and photos in VaultSync, decryption occurs dynamically in volatile memory (RAM) via Android's MediaDataSource. Plain-text versions of your encrypted media are never written to temporary flash storage cache files, eliminating forensic file recovery vulnerabilities.

4. Local Wi-Fi PC Sync Transport

The PC Sync feature operates entirely across your local area network (LAN) via a local HTTP daemon running on your phone. No files are routed through third-party cloud relays or WAN internet servers. Authentication is established directly through a dynamic, cryptographically generated QR code displayed on your device.

5. Requested Permissions & Purpose

  • Manage External Storage (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to organize files in your shared storage folder (Documents/VaultSync/) to guarantee vault survival across app uninstalls.
  • Camera: Used exclusively to scan on-screen pairing QR codes when connecting to local PC sync. No camera images or video feeds are ever stored or transmitted.
  • Foreground Service & WakeLock: Keeps background transfers active during high-bandwidth media streaming over local Wi-Fi without connection dropouts.
  • Biometric Authentication: Interfaces with Android's system BiometricPrompt API to verify your fingerprint or face locally before unwrapping KeyStore keys.

6. Open Source Code Transparency

VaultSync's complete source code is publicly accessible on GitHub (github.com/shintopc/VaultSync) for independent security review and cryptographic auditing.
