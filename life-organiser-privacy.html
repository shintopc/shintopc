Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 15, 2026

01 Introduction

Welcome to Life Organiser. Your privacy is our absolute priority. This application is designed as a secure, offline-first personal management tool. We believe that your personal data—finances, passwords, tasks, and documents—belongs solely to you.

02 Data Collection & Usage

Life Organiser stores all sensitive data locally on your device. We do not operate central servers that store your passwords or financial records.

  • Biometric Data: The app uses system-level Biometric APIs (Fingerprint/Face Unlock). We never see or store your biometric data; the authentication is handled entirely by your device's secure hardware.
  • Finance & Tasks: All records created within these modules are stored in an encrypted local database.
  • Passwords: Credentials stored in the Password Vault are encrypted using industry-standard protocols before being saved to local storage.

03 Device Permissions

To provide its full suite of features, Life Organiser may request the following permissions:

  • Storage: Required to save and retrieve documents in the Documents module.
  • Biometrics: Required to secure the app and specific modules.
  • Notifications: Required for the Reminders module.

04 Security Measures

Life Organiser includes several unique privacy features:

  • Screenshot Protection: By default, the app disables screenshots and hides content from the "Recent Apps" switcher to prevent accidental exposure of your data.
  • Auto-Lock: The app automatically re-locks upon being sent to the background or after a period of inactivity.
