Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 15, 2026

1. Overview

SportField Pro is committed to protecting the privacy of sports facilities and athletes. This policy explains how we handle your facility data, booking records, and user information.

2. Data Collection & Storage

All sensitive operational data, including ground schedules, pricing, and member lists, are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your business data to our servers unless you explicitly use a cloud-sync feature (if available).

3. Location Services

SportField Pro may request access to your device's location to help athletes find nearby sports facilities. This data is processed locally and is not stored by ShintoPC Labs.

4. Permissions

  • Calendar: To sync facility bookings with your device calendar.
  • Storage: To export booking reports and invoices as PDF files.
  • Camera: Optional, to upload facility photos or player profile pictures.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact ShintoPC Labs via the official website support channels.
