App-Specific Policy

CATERPRO
PRIVACY

Effective Date: April 2026. This policy outlines how CaterPro Calc manages your business data, recipes, and contact information.

1. Data Storage

All recipes, ingredient lists, event details, and catalogs are stored locally on your device using an encrypted database. We do not have access to your business data.

2. Backup and Sync

If you use the Backup/Restore feature, your data is exported to a file which you control. ShintoPC Labs does not provide cloud-based backup services for CaterPro Calc; you are responsible for the security of your backup files.

3. Permissions

  • Storage/Files: Required to generate and save PDF proposals and purchase lists.
  • Contacts/Sharing: Used only when you explicitly choose to share a list via WhatsApp or email.

4. Security

We use modern Android security protocols to ensure that other applications on your device cannot access the CaterPro Calc database.
