Uncompromising Privacy

The 6 Pillars of Security

Engineered from the silicon up to guarantee your personal media never leaks, is never tracked, and remains under your absolute physical custody.

cloud_off

100% Offline & Zero Cloud

Contains no analytics SDKs, zero third-party telemetry, and zero remote cloud dependencies. Your media files and cryptographic master keys never leave your physical Android device.

enhanced_encryption

Hardware KeyStore (StrongBox)

Employs chunked AES-256-GCM authenticated envelope encryption. Encryption keys are generated and sealed directly inside your device's hardware TEE or StrongBox enclave.

memory

RAM-Only Video Decryption

In-memory streaming decryption via Android MediaDataSource extracts video and photo thumbnails strictly in volatile RAM. No unencrypted temporary files are ever written to flash memory.

wifi_tethering

Local Wi-Fi PC Sync

Zero-cloud desktop browser access over your local Wi-Fi. Features foreground service with CPU WakeLock and Wi-Fi Lock, paired instantly using an on-screen QR code without typing credentials.

masks

Calculator & Notes Stealth

Camouflage the app icon as a functional Calculator or Notes app. Background alias synchronization prevents OS process kills while secret gestures or dialer codes open the real vault.

inventory_2

Uninstall Survival & Recovery

Safe storage in Documents/VaultSync/ with encrypted manifests (vault_manifest.vsm) ensures your vault survives app reinstalls and OS updates with zero duplicate re-encryption.

Anti-Lockout Guarantee

Never Lose Access to Your Memories

Traditional vaults create fatal lockouts if users forget their PIN or camouflage gestures. VaultSync incorporates dual recovery systems:

key

128-Bit Master Recovery Key

Generated offline during initial vault setup. Keep this printed or stored in your password manager to bypass Master PIN lockouts without compromising underlying file encryption.

dialpad

Emergency Dialer & Deep Link

If disguise modes (Calculator/Notes) ever lose secret gesture response, dialing *#*#82858#*#* or opening the private intent link restores the master gateway.
